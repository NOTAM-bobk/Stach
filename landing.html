Core Features

Frequently asked questions

Is my data actually private?
Absolutely. Everything you save is private by default. We do not sell data, nor do we run ads. You can explicitly choose to create a public folder to share with a team, but otherwise everything stays private.
Can I import from my browser or other tools?
Yes. We support standard HTML bookmark exports from Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge. Just head over to your Settings page after creating an account to upload your file.
Is there a mobile app available?
Our web app is fully optimized for mobile browsers and can be saved to your home screen as a PWA. Native iOS and Android apps are currently in private beta.
